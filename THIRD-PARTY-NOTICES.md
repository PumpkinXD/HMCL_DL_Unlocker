execjar is invoked **at build time** to generate the `.sh` artifact: it
prepends a POSIX `sh` stub and fixes the JAR's internal zip offsets.

**execjar itself is not redistributed** — no part of the execjar program is
copied into our artifacts. The shell stub in the `.sh` artifact is generated
output (a rendered template), not a copy of execjar source. It is listed here
for completeness and transparency, not out of a strict obligation.
